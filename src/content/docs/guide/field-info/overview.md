**Field info** is about the place where you are standing. It has three tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Areas** | The WWFF references around you. See [Areas nearby](/guide/field-info/areas-nearby/). |
| **Location** | Where you are in radio terms: time, locator, zones. See [Location in radio terms](/guide/field-info/location/). |
| **Conditions** | Space weather and what it means for the bands. See [Space weather](/guide/field-info/conditions-space-weather/) and [The band bars](/guide/field-info/conditions-bands/). |

## Opening Field info

1. Tap **Field info** in the bottom bar (the circle with a dot).
2. Tap the tab you want at the top: **Areas**, **Location** or **Conditions**.
3. Under the title, check where Diana starts from: *"from your GPS position"* or *"from your locator JO21EE"*.

:::caution[No position?]
If you see *"Diana does not know where you are yet"*, allow location (see [Welcome screen](/guide/start/welcome-screen/)) or fill in your locator under **Settings** › **Your station**. With only a locator, Diana works from the centre of that square, which can be a few kilometres off.
:::
