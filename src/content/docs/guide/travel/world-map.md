Zoom out on the map and you see the whole world: every WWFF reference, more than 65,000 of them, as blue points.

## How it works

- Points close together form a **blue circle with a number**. Tap it to zoom in; the circle splits apart.
- From zoom level **11**, every point gets its reference as a label.
- **Tap a point** for its panel: reference, country and name, locator and zones, bearing and distance from you, QSO count or **ATNO**, and More info.
- For a country with boundaries that is not loaded, the panel offers **Show [country] in detail**.

## Switching the world off

Under **≡** › **Other WWFF areas** you hide the blue points, for a calmer map around your own country.

:::tip[Hunting for ATNO]
Open the Spots screen with **Worldwide**: an **ATNO** in red is a reference that was never activated. Tap it to see where it is.
:::
