Diana works everywhere: every WWFF reference in the world is on the map. What changes abroad is whether Diana has the real boundaries of that country.

## Before you leave

1. Open **Settings** › **Your country** and choose the country you will visit.
2. Read the line under the list:
   - **With boundaries**: the areas appear in full, and Diana can tell whether you are inside.
   - **Points only**: you see the references as points at the position from the WWFF directory.
3. Open the map once with signal, at home or at the hotel, and zoom around the area where you will go. Diana keeps what it loaded for offline use.
4. Under **Settings** › **Field info**, a larger distance (50 or 100 km) often helps in a country with fewer references.

## In a country with boundaries

Everything works as at home: areas, *am I inside*, overlap and the distance to the boundary. Today that is Belgium, the Netherlands, Germany, Denmark and Portugal, and more are added all the time.

## In a country with points only

- You see where references are, and you get their details by tapping: name, locator, zones, QSO count or ATNO, bearing and distance, and More info. See [Points and areas](/guide/map/points-and-areas/).
- Diana cannot tell whether you are inside, because it does not know the boundary. Check the official map of that national programme, or the website under **More info**.
- Spotting yourself and announcing work exactly as at home.

:::tip[The spots filter]
Want to see only the spots of the country you visit? Choose it under **Show** in Settings, or on the Spots screen. Choosing another country under **Country** never changes that filter by itself.
:::

:::caution[Your licence abroad]
Diana does not check whether you may transmit in another country. Check the rules for operating abroad (for example CEPT) before you go.
:::
