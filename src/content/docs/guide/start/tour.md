After the wizard, Diana asks: *"A short tour of the screens?"* Tap **Start the tour** and Diana opens each screen in turn, with a short card that explains what you see.

The tour covers, in this order:

- **Map**: tapping Map centres it on you, green areas, the green dotted line to a spot, the orange dashed line to an announcement, and the buttons at the top.
- **Spots**: On air, Agenda, your country or Worldwide, and tapping a spot.
- **Spot**: spotting yourself, the reference filled in for you, *Check* before *Send*, and the quick words.
- **Agenda**: announcing an activation and keeping the PIN.
- **Field info**: Areas, Location and Conditions.
- **Rules**: the WWFF rules and the band plan.
- **Settings**: everything you just set, and where to run the wizard again.

You can stop the tour at any moment. Missed it? Run the wizard again under **Settings** › **Run the setup wizard again**; at the end it offers the tour once more.
