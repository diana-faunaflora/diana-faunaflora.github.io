Right after the welcome screen, a short wizard sets up Diana for your station. It takes about a minute. Every answer is saved the moment you give it, so stopping halfway loses nothing.

![Step 2 of the wizard: your station, with callsign, portable callsign and locator](../../../../assets/screens/phone-wizard-station.webp)

## The steps

1. **Language.** The wizard starts in English. Tap your language: EN, NL, FR, DE, DA, IT, ES or PT. Everything switches at once.
2. **Your station.**
   - **Callsign**: your normal callsign, for example `ON3VZ`. Diana uses it as the spotter, and it decides which country Diana picks for you.
   - **Portable callsign**: what you use in the field, for example `ON3VZ/P`. Diana fills it in as the activator when you spot yourself or announce.
   - **Maidenhead locator**: six characters, for example `JO21EE`. Tap **Take from GPS** to fill it in from your position. As long as there is no GPS position, Diana starts from your locator: for the map at startup, the distances in Field info and the lines to the spots.
3. **Your country.** Which country Diana loads with its real boundaries. Diana proposes one based on your callsign. See [Your country](/guide/settings/your-country/).
4. **Quick words.** Five words you can add to a spot’s remark with one tap. Tap **Fill in example words** for a start, or leave them empty: an empty field simply gives no button.
5. **Band list.** **All bands** (160 m to 23 cm) or **HF only** (80, 40, 30, 20, 15 and 10 m) for the list when you announce an activation.
6. **Statistics.** Only if you did not say yes yet: a second, polite question whether you want to help with anonymous statistics.
7. **Done.** Your settings are saved. They stay in the browser of this device as long as you do not clear the browser data.

Use **Next** and **Back** to move between the steps. **Skip introduction** stops the wizard; you can always fill in the rest under Settings.

:::note
Everything you fill in here can be changed later under **Settings**. There you can also run the wizard again: **Settings** › **Run the setup wizard again**.
:::
