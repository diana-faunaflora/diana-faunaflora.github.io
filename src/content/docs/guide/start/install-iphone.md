On iPhone and iPad you install Diana from **Safari**. Apple only allows web apps to be installed from Safari, so Chrome or Firefox on an iPhone cannot do it.

## Step by step

1. Open **Safari** and go to **diana-onff.github.io**.
2. Tap the **Share** button **⬆︎** (a square with an arrow pointing up). On an iPhone it is at the bottom of the screen, on an iPad at the top right.
3. Scroll down in the list that opens and tap **Add to Home Screen**. Don’t see it? Scroll all the way down and tap **Edit Actions** to add it.
4. Leave the name as **Diana** and tap **Add** at the top right.
5. Diana now has its own icon on your home screen. Open it from there from now on.

:::caution[Opened Diana in Chrome on your iPhone?]
Then Diana says: *"On iPhone and iPad this only works from Safari."* Copy the address, open it in Safari, and follow the steps above.
:::

## Location on iPhone

Give Safari permission to use your precise location, otherwise Diana gets only a rough position:

1. Open the **Settings** app of your iPhone.
2. Go to **Privacy & Security** › **Location Services** › **Safari Websites**.
3. Choose **While Using the App** and switch on **Precise Location**.

More about this in [Location problems](/guide/help/location-problems/).

## Removing it

Hold the Diana icon and choose **Remove App** › **Delete from Home Screen**. Your settings in Diana are then gone too.
