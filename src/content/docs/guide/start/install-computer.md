On a computer you can use Diana straight in the browser. Installing gives it its own window and an icon in the taskbar or Dock.

## Chrome or Edge (Windows, macOS, Linux)

1. Open **diana-onff.github.io**.
2. Click the **install icon** at the right of the address bar (a screen with a small arrow), or open the menu **⋮** and choose **Install Diana**.
3. Click **Install**.

You can also do it from inside Diana: **Settings** › **Install as an app** › **Install on this device**.

## Safari on a Mac

1. Open **diana-onff.github.io** in Safari.
2. In the menu bar choose **File** › **Add to Dock** (macOS Sonoma or newer).
3. Click **Add**.

## Firefox

Firefox cannot install web apps. Diana works fine in Firefox, but as a normal tab. Make a bookmark, or open Diana in Chrome or Edge if you want an installed app.

:::note[Location on a computer]
A laptop or desktop has no GPS. The position comes from wifi and is rarely better than about ±50 m. For inside-or-outside questions, use your phone in the field. At home, the locator in Settings is good enough.
:::
