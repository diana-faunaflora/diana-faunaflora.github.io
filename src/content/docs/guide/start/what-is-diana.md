Diana is a free map app for radio amateurs in WWFF, World Wide Flora & Fauna. It shows the nature areas you can activate, with their real boundaries, and who is on the air from those areas right now.

## Who it is for

- **Activators**: you take a portable station into a nature area. Diana shows where the area is, whether you are really inside it, and lets you spot yourself.
- **Hunters**: you work the activators from home. Diana shows who is on the air, where they are, and whether a reference was ever activated before.

## What you can do with it

- See every WWFF reference in the world on one map, and the areas of five countries with their real boundaries.
- Find out whether you are inside an area, and how far the nearest boundary is.
- See who is on the air now, and who announced an activation.
- Spot yourself and announce an activation through WWFF Spotline.
- Look up your locator, CQ zone and ITU zone, and today’s band conditions.

## What Diana is, and what it is not

- **A web app.** It runs in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. You can install it so it gets its own icon, but you do not need an app store.
- **Free and without an account.** Everything you fill in stays on your own device.
- **A volunteer project.** Diana supports the WWFF hobby. It is not an official WWFF service, and it is not for commercial use.
