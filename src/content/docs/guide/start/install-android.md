On Android, Diana installs like an ordinary app: an icon on your home screen, its own window, and it keeps working without signal.

## The quickest way: from inside Diana

1. Open **diana-onff.github.io** in **Chrome** (Edge and Samsung Internet work too).
2. Tap **Settings** at the bottom right (the gear).
3. Scroll to **Install as an app** and tap **Install on this device**.
4. Your phone asks for confirmation. Tap **Install**.
5. Diana now has its own icon on your home screen. Open it from there from now on.

:::tip
The first time you open the map, Diana may also show a bar at the bottom: *"Put Diana on this device as an app?"*. Tap **Install** there and you are done too. Tap **Not now** and the bar does not come back; the button in Settings is always there.
:::

## Through the browser menu

If you do not see the install button, use Chrome’s own menu:

1. Open **diana-onff.github.io** in Chrome.
2. Tap the three dots **⋮** at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app** (the name depends on your Chrome version).
4. Confirm with **Install** or **Add**.

## How do I know it worked?

- In Settings, the install card now reads **"Diana is installed on this device"**.
- Diana opens without the address bar of the browser.

## Removing it

Hold the Diana icon on your home screen and choose **Uninstall** or **Remove**. Note: this also removes your settings, such as your callsign and locator. See [Your data and the limits](/guide/help/data-and-limits/).
