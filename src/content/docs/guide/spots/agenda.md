On the **Spots** screen, tap the tab **Agenda**. You see every announced activation, with reference, bands and modes, and date and time in UTC.

![The Agenda tab with announced activations](../../../../assets/screens/phone-agenda.webp)

## Reading the agenda

- **Running now**: an orange dot and *"live until …"*.
- **Still to come**: grey, with date and start time.
- **ATNO** in red: the reference was never activated.

Your choice of **your country** or **Worldwide** applies here too.

## Opening an announcement

Tap a line. The map opens on that area with band and mode, the reserve, the locator, the remark and the bearing. **← Back to Spots** takes you back.

## Announcing yourself

Tap **+ Announce** at the top of the agenda. See [Announce an activation](/guide/spots/announce/).

:::tip[On the map too]
Announcements are on the map as a white dot with an orange ring, with an orange dashed line from you. See [Reading the map](/guide/map/reading-the-map/).
:::
