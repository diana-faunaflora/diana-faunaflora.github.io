On the air and ready? Spot yourself, so hunters can find you. The spot goes to WWFF Spotline and from there to the DX cluster.

![The Spot screen with reference, callsigns, frequency, mode and remark](../../../../assets/screens/phone-spot-yourself.webp)

## Step by step

1. Tap **Spot** in the bottom bar, or **Spot yourself here** in the panel of an area.
2. Check the **reference**. If you are inside an area, or came from a panel, Diana has already filled it in and says where it came from.
3. Your **callsigns** come from Settings: the portable callsign as activator, your normal callsign as spotter.
4. Fill in the **frequency** in kHz, for example `7144`, and choose the **mode**.
5. Optional: a short **remark**, up to 100 characters. Tap one of your **quick words** to add it with one tap.
6. Tap **Check**. Spotline looks at your spot without placing it, and tells you what it thinks.
7. Is it accepted? Tap **Send spot**. You get a confirmation with the number of your spot.

## Why first Check, then Send?

Every request to Spotline comes out of a budget shared by all users. *Check* makes sure a spot is right before it costs a real send. Change anything after checking, and *Send* closes again: the approval was for the spot you had then.

## What Diana checks for you

- The **callsign** format, the **frequency** range and the **remark** length, just like Spotline.
- The **band plan**: a warning when your mode does not match the segment of your frequency.
- The **reference**, against Diana’s own list. That answers at once, even without signal.

:::caution[What does not belong in a remark]
No thank-yous or band requests. Every spot goes to the DX cluster, where that is noise. Spotline also filters inappropriate language.
:::

## When something goes wrong

- **Pressed twice on a bad connection?** Diana says the spot was already there. Nothing is placed twice.
- **Spotline refuses?** You get Spotline’s own explanation.
- **Sending does not work at all?** Diana offers **Send the old way**: the spot goes to Spotline’s own form in a new tab. You see their answer yourself, but Diana cannot confirm it.
