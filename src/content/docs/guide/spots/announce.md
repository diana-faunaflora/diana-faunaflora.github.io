Planning an activation? Announce it, so hunters are ready. The announcement goes to the WWFF agenda on Spotline.

![The Announce screen with callsigns, reference, start and end, bands and modes](../../../../assets/screens/phone-announce.webp)

## Three ways in

- **Agenda** in the bottom bar.
- **+ Announce** on the Agenda tab of the Spots screen.
- **Announce an activation** in the panel of an area: the reference is then already filled in.

## Step by step

1. Check the **activator’s callsign** and **your own callsign**. Someone else may announce for an activator.
2. Fill in the **WWFF reference**, for example `ONFF-0104`.
3. Choose **start** and **end**. You pick them on the clock of your own device; Diana converts them to UTC before they leave your device.
4. Optional: tick the **bands** and **modes** (several are possible, for example *40 m, 20 m · CW, SSB*) and add a remark.
5. Tap **Check**, and when it is accepted, **Send announcement**.
6. **Write down your PIN.**

:::danger[Keep your PIN]
The PIN is the only way to change or cancel this announcement later, on Spotline’s own site. Diana cannot do that for you, and a lost PIN cannot be recovered. That is why Diana keeps a list under the form of everything you announced from this device (reference, date, PIN). Tap **✕** to remove a line. That list lives only on this device.
:::

## Limits

- At most **one month** ahead, and the end not before the start. Spotline applies the same rules.
- At most **16 announcements a day** from one device through Diana, to protect the shared budget. Normal use stays far below that.
- There is no "old way" for announcements. If it does not work, try again later.

Which bands the list offers (all, or HF only) is a setting: [Band list when announcing](/guide/settings/band-list/).
