Tap **Rules** in the bottom bar. The tab **Rules** opens: a selection from the official WWFF rules that matter most in the field, in your language.

![The Rules screen with the rules about the reference area](../../../../assets/screens/phone-rules.webp)

## What is in it

The rules 3.5, 3.6, 4.4, 4.7, 6.8, 6.9 and 6.11 of the WWFF Global Rules, in three groups:

- **The area**: being inside the reference area, references bordering or containing another one, activating several at once.
- **QSOs**: rules about the contacts themselves.
- **Logs**: how to name and upload your log.

They are WWFF rules, so they apply to ONFF and every other national programme too.

:::note[Translations]
The texts in other languages are translated by the Diana team. The English text of WWFF applies. Each translation links to the official PDF.
:::

## The name of your log file

Rule 6.8 asks for a fixed file name. Diana shows an example with every part labelled:

`ON3VZ@ONFF-0104 20261002.adi`

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ON3VZ` | Your callsign. |
| `@` | Separator. |
| `ONFF-0104` | The reference. |
| (space) | One space. |
| `20261002` | The date: year, month, day. |
| `.adi` | An ADIF log file. |

Rule 6.9 points to the official log search of WWFF: [wwff.co/logsearch](https://wwff.co/logsearch/).

:::tip
Diana does not log for you. It helps you before and during the activation; your log stays in your own logging program.
:::
