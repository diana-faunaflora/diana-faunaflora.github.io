On the **Rules** screen, tap the tab **Band plan**.

![The band plan with the CW, digital and SSB segments of 80, 40 and 30 m](../../../../assets/screens/phone-bandplan.webp)

## Reading it

For each band:

- A **bar** with the segments for **CW**, **digital** and **SSB**, with their frequencies, for example *CW 3.500 to 3.570 MHz*.
- The **FT8** frequency, marked.
- The **WWFF preferred frequencies**, highlighted in a yellow box, for example *SSB 7.144 · CW 7.024*.

## Why it matters

When you spot yourself, Diana checks your frequency against this band plan and warns you if your mode does not fit the segment. See [Spot yourself](/guide/spots/spot-yourself/).

:::tip
On a busy activation day, start near the WWFF preferred frequency: that is where hunters listen first.
:::
