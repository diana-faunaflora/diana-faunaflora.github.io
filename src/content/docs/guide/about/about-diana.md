## Why Diana?

Diana is the Roman goddess of the hunt and of the forests. That is exactly what WWFF is about: nature areas, and hunters chasing every new reference. The deer with the bow in the logo refers to her.

## Who makes it

Diana is a volunteer project, built as a hobby by radio amateurs, to support WWFF activity. It is not an official WWFF service; it only uses their public services.

## For national coordinators: getting your boundaries in Diana

Diana shows real boundaries wherever a national programme supplies an area file. To add your country, or update it:

1. Prepare your area file as **KMZ**, following the [KMZ format guide (PDF)](https://diana-onff.github.io/kmz-guide.pdf).
2. Send it to **wwffmembers@gmail.com**.
3. The Diana team checks it, does the first upload itself, and releases it when everything is right.
